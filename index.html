02 — About

A developer who builds for the business behind the website.

I'm Abiola Olomola, a freelance web developer based in Lagos, Nigeria. I build websites for businesses, brands, organisations and individuals — and I build them end to end: structure, layout, styling, interaction and responsive behaviour.

My work starts with understanding what a business actually needs the site to do. A car dealership needs browsable inventory and a fast enquiry path. A school needs to feel trustworthy and make applying simple. A fashion label needs product pages and a cart that doesn't get in the way. The design decisions follow from that, not the other way around.

Everything in this portfolio is hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no page builders, no templates. The concept demos and live websites in the portfolio are the clearest explanation of what I do: different industries, different design languages, one developer.

About us

A school built one child at a time

Gems Land Private School opened in 2009 with two classrooms, fourteen pupils and a simple belief: that children learn best when they feel safe, known and genuinely challenged. Sixteen years later the belief hasn't changed — only the number of children we serve.

We are a day school in Lagos offering Creche through Senior Secondary. Our curriculum follows the Nigerian national curriculum, enriched with ICT, French, Yoruba, coding, music and sport. Class sizes are capped at 18 and each class has a form teacher who is responsible for knowing every pupil individually.

We do not claim to be the biggest or the most expensive school in Lagos. We claim to be a school where your child will be seen.

FAQ

Questions parents ask us

What ages do you accept?

We accept children from three months old (Creche) through to Senior Secondary. Entry points are Creche, Pre-Nursery, Nursery 1, Primary 1, JSS 1 and SS 1, though we admit into other classes where space allows.

What is the school calendar?

We run a three-term academic year following the Lagos State Ministry of Education calendar. Terms typically run September–December, January–April and April–July.

Do you provide school bus service?

Yes. We operate school buses on several routes across the mainland and island. Routes and fees are confirmed at enrolment — contact the office for current coverage.

Are meals provided?

A hot lunch is provided daily and included in the termly fee. Vegetarian and allergy-specific requirements can be accommodated — please indicate these on the application form.

How do you communicate with parents?

Through a parent portal, a termly newsletter, class WhatsApp groups and scheduled parent-teacher conferences. Teachers are also reachable by appointment.

What documents are required for admission?

Birth certificate or age declaration, two passport photographs, the child's most recent school report (where applicable) and a transfer certificate for pupils moving from another school.

We started
with 12 shirts.

D'Art Original began as a side project: twelve adire shirts, dyed in a friend's backyard in Abeokuta and sold to people we knew in Lagos. They sold out in nine days, which was more surprising to us than to anyone else.

We now work with a team of eight tailors and two dyers, and we still produce in runs of 40 to 120 pieces. Small runs mean we can inspect every garment, correct problems before they reach you, and keep the quantities honest.

We don't release seasonal collections on a fashion calendar. We release things when they're right, and we restock what people keep asking for.

Everything is cut, sewn and finished in Lagos, Nigeria.

Size guide

Measurements in centimetres

Our garments are cut with a relaxed, unisex fit. If you're between sizes, take the smaller size for a closer fit or the larger size for a looser, boxier silhouette. All measurements are of the garment laid flat, not of the body.

How to measure

  • Chest — measure across the widest part of the garment, armpit to armpit, then double it.
  • Length — from the highest point of the shoulder to the bottom hem.
  • Shoulder — seam to seam across the top of the back.
  • Sleeve — from the shoulder seam to the end of the cuff.

Exchanges

If the size isn't right, you can exchange within 14 days of delivery provided the item is unworn, unwashed and still has its tags. Adire pieces can be exchanged but not refunded, because each one is dyed individually.

To arrange an exchange, message us on WhatsApp with your order reference.

About

Twenty-eight seats and one kitchen

We opened in 2019 with a short menu and a long prep list. That hasn't really changed.

Lagos Table started because we wanted somewhere to eat that felt like a good Nigerian home kitchen — but with proper service and a wine list. The food is Nigerian: soups, grills, rice dishes, small chops. The approach is restaurant: consistent portions, careful plating, everything served hot and on time.

Our pepper base is blended every morning. Our stock is cooked from bones over six hours. Our suya spice is mixed in-house and toasted weekly. Our jollof is cooked over firewood in a party-size pot, then finished to order in smaller batches so it never sits.

Chef Adeola Bello trained in Lagos and worked in Accra for four years before returning. She has run this kitchen since the day it opened.

FAQ

Questions we get asked

How do I book a shipment?

Request a quote through the contact form, message us on WhatsApp, or call the office. For business accounts, collections can be scheduled on a recurring basis. Once a booking is confirmed you'll receive a tracking number by email and SMS.

What are your delivery timelines?

Same-day within Lagos for parcels collected before 11:00am. Lagos to Abuja and Port Harcourt is next-day. Lagos to Kano, Kaduna and Maiduguri is 48 hours. Remote destinations may take an additional day.

How is my shipment insured?

Standard shipments are covered up to ₦500,000 at no extra cost. Interstate freight is covered up to ₦5,000,000. Higher-value cargo can be insured at additional cost — declare the value when booking.

What items can't you ship?

We don't transport live animals, unlicensed firearms, narcotics, hazardous chemicals without documentation, or cash. Restricted items requiring permits must have paperwork provided before collection.

Do you offer cash-on-delivery?

Yes, for registered business accounts. We collect payment from the recipient, remit to you weekly, and provide a full reconciliation with each remittance.

How do I open a business account?

Submit the business enquiry form. We'll arrange a call to understand your volumes and destinations, then send a rate card. Accounts are typically activated within two working days.

What happens if a shipment is delayed?

You'll see the delay in the tracking timeline and we'll contact you proactively if it affects the promised delivery window. Delays caused by us are credited against your next invoice.

Can I change the delivery address after dispatch?

Yes, before the shipment reaches its destination city. There may be a redirection charge depending on the distance. Contact us with the tracking number as soon as possible.
